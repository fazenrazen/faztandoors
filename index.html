How it works

Same four days, every week

MON — TUE

Order

The board opens Monday morning and closes Tuesday at 9pm. We only buy what's been claimed, so nothing is wasted and nothing sits.

WEDNESDAY

Marinate

Thighs get weighed, bagged and left in yogurt and spice overnight — fourteen hours minimum. This part can't be rushed, which is why there are no Thursday walk-ups.

THURSDAY PM

Fire

The tandoor is lit once and runs about four hours. Everything cooks in sequence and goes straight into the pack — it is never cooled and reheated.

THU 5 — 6:30

Collect

Pick a half-hour window at checkout. We text when yours comes off the coals. Collection only, in Boulder.

Questions

The ones worth asking

Why January?

Colorado's expanded cottage food law — the one that lets a home kitchen sell meat — takes effect on 1 January 2027. We could rent a commercial kitchen and start sooner, but that cost would land on the price of the food. We'd rather wait and charge you $55 instead of $75.

Why boneless thighs and not a whole bird?

Thighs stay juicy over high heat in a way breast never does, they take marinade all the way through, and there's no bone in the weight you're paying for. Two pounds of thigh genuinely feeds four. A whole bird looks more impressive and feeds fewer people.

Where is it actually cooked?

In our home kitchen in Boulder. That kitchen is not licensed or inspected by the state, which is what the cottage food law permits and what we're required to tell you plainly. We hold current food safety certification, and every pack is labelled with its cook date and lot number.

Can you deliver?

No, and not through DoorDash either — cottage food has to go from us straight to you, so collection is the only way this works. Thursday evening, half-hour windows, in Boulder.

Is there rice or dal?

Not at first. The law lets us sell one refrigerated food type and we've spent it on the chicken. Naan, lime achar and dry chaat masala are shelf-stable so those we can sell alongside — bring your own rice.

What about allergies?

The marinade contains dairy. The kitchen also handles wheat, mustard, sesame and tree nuts, and we can't guarantee separation. Full ingredient list is printed on every pack. Tell us at checkout and we'll tell you honestly whether we can serve you safely.

How long does it keep?

Three days in the fridge below 41°F. It is cooked once and never reheated by us — what you collect came off the coals that afternoon.
